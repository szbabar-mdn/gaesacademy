About GAES

Global Academic Evaluation Services (GAES) is dedicated to elevating academic assessment standards across secondary and higher secondary education. By delivering rigorous, board-standard pre-evaluations, we bridge the gap between classroom instruction and formal board examination readiness.

Our system moves academic decision-making from guesswork to actionable data, helping both students and educators target key areas for improvement before official examinations take place.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, standardized pre-board evaluations that offer precise insights into student performance, fostering academic excellence, reducing examination stress, and facilitating targeted instruction for SSC and HSSC students.

Our Vision

To be the premier educational evaluation body recognized globally for promoting academic integrity, empowering educational institutions, and maximizing student achievement through data-driven assessment strategies.
